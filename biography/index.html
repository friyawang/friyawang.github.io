Biography个人简介

I am an associate professor in the School of Integrated Circuit Science and Engineering at Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications and a member of the Power Micro-Electronics Lab, led by Prof. Yufeng Guo. I received my bachelor's degree in microelectronic science and engineering from Xidian University in 2020 and my Ph.D. from Fudan University in 2025 under Prof. Xiaoyang Zeng.

My research interests are power-management IC design and AI for electronic design automation. My chip design experience spans modeling, system architecture, transistor-level and layout design, tape-out, and measurement. I have published as a first or corresponding author in JSSC, TVLSI, TPEL, and TCAS-II, and at ICCAD.

王静，南京邮电大学集成电路科学与工程学院副教授，郭宇锋教授负责的功率微电子实验室（PMEL）成员。2020 年获西安电子科技大学微电子科学与工程专业学士学位，2025 年获复旦大学博士学位，师从曾晓洋教授。

主要研究电源管理芯片设计与大模型辅助的电子设计自动化。具有模拟与电源管理芯片从理论建模、系统架构、晶体管级电路与版图设计到流片测试的完整研发经验。以第一作者或通讯作者在 JSSC、TVLSI、TPEL、TCAS-II、ICCAD 等期刊和会议发表论文。
