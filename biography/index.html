Biography个人简介

Jing Wang is a tenure-track associate professor. She received her bachelor's degree from the School of Microelectronics at Xidian University in 2020 and her Ph.D. from the School of Microelectronics at Fudan University in 2025, advised by Prof. Xiaoyang Zeng. In July 2025, she joined the Power Micro-Electronics Lab (led by Prof. Yufeng Guo) in the School of Integrated Circuit Science and Engineering at Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications.

She leads a Youth Project of the Natural Science Foundation of Jiangsu Province and an open sub-project of a national key laboratory, and participates as a core member in several projects of the National Key R&D Program of China. Her research focuses on analog integrated circuit design and its automation. She has completed multiple successful tape-outs, and has published more than ten papers as first or corresponding author in leading journals and conferences in integrated circuit design, including JSSC, TPEL, TVLSI, TCAS-II, and ICCAD.

王静，预聘副教授。2020 年和 2025 年于西安电子科技大学和复旦大学微电子学院分别获得学士和博士学位（导师：曾晓洋教授）。2025 年 7 月加入南京邮电大学集成电路科学与工程学院功率微电子实验室（负责人：郭宇锋教授）。

现主持江苏省自然科学基金青年基金项目、全国重点实验室开放子课题等，并作为核心技术骨干参与多项国家重点研发计划项目。长期从事模拟集成电路设计及其自动化研究，完成多次成功流片验证，并以第一/通讯作者在 JSSC、TPEL、TVLSI、TCAS-II、ICCAD 等集成电路设计领域国际顶级期刊和会议发表学术论文 10 余篇。
