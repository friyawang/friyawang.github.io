Biography个人简介

Background, education, teaching, and academic service.学术背景、教育与工作经历、教学与学术服务。

I am an Associate Professor in the School of Integrated Circuit Science and Engineering at Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications (NJUPT), and a member of the Power Micro-Electronics Lab (PMEL) led by Prof. Yufeng Guo. I received my Ph.D. in IC and System Design from Fudan University in 2025, advised by Prof. Xiaoyang Zeng, and my B.E. in Microelectronic Science and Engineering from Xidian University in 2020.

My research centers on power-management IC design, including LDOs, DC–DC converters with high conversion ratios (e.g., 12 V to 1 V), and microwatt-level low-power circuits. I have carried chip designs through the complete flow, from theoretical modeling and system architecture to transistor-level and layout design, tape-out, and measurement.

Building on this design experience, I also work on AI for electronic design automation, studying how large language models can represent circuit knowledge and support circuit retrieval, optimization, and verification. I have published as first or corresponding author in IEEE JSSC, TVLSI, TPEL, and TCAS-II and at ICCAD, and my collaborative work has appeared at ICML.

王静，南京邮电大学集成电路科学与工程学院副教授，郭宇锋教授负责的功率微电子实验室（PMEL）成员。2025 年获复旦大学集成电路与系统设计专业博士学位，师从曾晓洋教授；2020 年获西安电子科技大学微电子科学与工程专业学士学位。

主要研究方向为电源管理芯片设计，包括低压差线性稳压器（LDO）、高降压比 DC–DC 变换器（如 12 V 至 1 V）以及微瓦级低功耗电路。具有从理论建模、系统架构、晶体管级电路与版图设计到流片测试的完整芯片研发经验。

在此基础上，同时开展人工智能辅助电子设计自动化研究，探索大语言模型如何表示电路知识，并用于电路检索、优化与验证。以第一作者或通讯作者在 IEEE JSSC、TVLSI、TPEL、TCAS-II 期刊及 ICCAD 会议发表论文，合作成果发表于 ICML。
