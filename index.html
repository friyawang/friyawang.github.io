About Us关于我们

We are a research group in the Power Micro-Electronics Lab (PMEL) at the School of Integrated Circuit Science and Engineering, Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications. Our work connects two areas that are usually studied apart: the craft of analog and power-management circuit design, and the new possibilities that large language models bring to electronic design automation.

On the circuit side, we design interface and power-conversion circuits that harvest ambient energy efficiently and deliver stable power to self-powered sensors and IoT devices, and we verify our ideas in silicon. On the AI side, we turn circuit knowledge into forms that models can learn from, and we build retrieval systems, design agents, and benchmarks that help engineers explore, optimize, and verify designs. Hands-on tape-out experience tells us which AI problems are worth solving, and the AI tools we build feed back into our own chip design.

我们是南京邮电大学集成电路科学与工程学院功率微电子实验室（PMEL）的研究团队，关注两个通常被分开研究的方向：模拟与电源管理芯片设计，以及大语言模型为电子设计自动化（EDA）带来的新机遇。

在电路方面，我们设计高效的能量采集接口与电源转换电路，为自供能传感器和物联网设备提供稳定的能量，并坚持通过流片与测试验证每一个想法。在人工智能方面，我们研究如何把电路知识表示成模型可学习的形式，构建电路检索系统、设计智能体与评测基准，帮助工程师更快地探索、优化和验证设计。真实的流片经验让我们找准值得用 AI 解决的问题，而我们开发的 AI 工具也反过来服务于自己的芯片设计。

Research Directions研究方向

Power Management & Energy-Harvesting ICs电源管理与能量采集芯片

Ambient energy such as mechanical vibration is weak and irregular. We design interface and conversion circuits that extract more of this energy and deliver regulated power with fewer off-chip components.振动等环境能量微弱且不稳定。我们设计能量采集接口与电源转换电路，以更少的片外元件获取更多能量，并输出稳定的电源。

  • Synchronized-switch rectifiers (SSHC, bias-flip) for piezoelectric energy harvesting面向压电能量采集的同步开关整流器（SSHC、偏置翻转）
  • Multi-input and multi-output DC–DC converters with shared capacitors共享电容的多输入、多输出 DC–DC 变换器
  • Maximum power point tracking and reconfigurable harvester arrays最大功率点跟踪与可重构能量采集阵列
  • Low-dropout regulators and on-chip power conditioning低压差线性稳压器（LDO）与片上电源调理

LLM-Driven Chip Design Automation大模型驱动的芯片设计自动化

Circuit design still depends heavily on expert experience. We study how to represent circuit knowledge so that large language models can retrieve, reason about, and optimize designs, and how to evaluate them on realistic design tasks.电路设计高度依赖专家经验。我们研究如何表示电路知识，使大模型能够检索、理解并优化电路，同时在真实设计任务上客观评估它们的能力。

  • Cross-modal representation learning and retrieval for analog circuits模拟电路的跨模态表示学习与检索
  • AI agents for automated analog circuit sizing and optimization智能体驱动的模拟电路参数设计与优化
  • IP-preserving RTL optimization with local–cloud LLM collaboration兼顾 IP 保护的本地–云端协同 RTL 优化
  • Benchmarks for long-horizon design agents, and AI for computational lithography长时程设计智能体评测基准与 AI 计算光刻
