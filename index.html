About Us关于我们

Our group works on analog integrated circuit design, focusing on power-management ICs that range from microwatt-level low-power circuits to high-power DC–DC converters. Our designs are taped out and measured.

We also study the application of large language models to electronic design automation, including circuit retrieval, design optimization, and verification.

课题组从事模拟集成电路设计，主要研究电源管理芯片，涵盖微瓦级低功耗电路到大功率 DC–DC 变换器，设计均经过流片与测试验证。

同时研究大语言模型在电子设计自动化中的应用，包括电路检索、设计优化与验证。

Research Directions研究方向

Power Management & Energy-Harvesting ICs电源管理与能量采集芯片

Analog and power-management IC design, from microwatt-level energy-harvesting interfaces to high-power DC–DC converters.模拟与电源管理芯片设计，涵盖微瓦级能量采集接口到大功率 DC–DC 变换器。

  • Synchronized-switch rectifiers (SSHC, bias-flip) for piezoelectric energy harvesting面向压电能量采集的同步开关整流器（SSHC、偏置翻转）
  • High-power DC–DC converters and multi-output DC–DC converters with shared capacitors大功率 DC–DC 变换器与共享电容多输出 DC–DC 变换器
  • Maximum power point tracking and reconfigurable harvester arrays最大功率点跟踪与可重构能量采集阵列
  • Low-dropout regulators and on-chip power conditioning低压差线性稳压器（LDO）与片上电源调理

LLM-Driven Chip Design Automation大模型驱动的芯片设计自动化

Applications of large language models in analog and digital circuit design automation.大语言模型在模拟与数字电路设计自动化中的应用。

  • Cross-modal representation learning and retrieval for analog circuits模拟电路的跨模态表示学习与检索
  • AI agents for automated analog circuit sizing and optimization智能体驱动的模拟电路参数设计与优化
  • IP-preserving RTL optimization with local–cloud LLM collaboration兼顾 IP 保护的本地–云端协同 RTL 优化
  • Benchmarks for long-horizon design agents, and AI for computational lithography长时程设计智能体评测基准与 AI 计算光刻
