About个人简介

I am an Associate Professor at Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications (NJUPT), and a member of the Power Micro-Electronics Lab (PMEL) led by Prof. Yufeng Guo. I received my Ph.D. from Fudan University, advised by Prof. Xiaoyang Zeng, and my bachelor's degree from Xidian University.

I design and test power-management ICs for piezoelectric energy harvesting. Our 180 nm CMOS chip uses a 37-phase rectifier and achieves a measured voltage-flipping efficiency of up to 83%.

I also study AI for electronic design automation. Our AnalogRetriever searches across schematics, SPICE netlists, and text, achieving 75.2% mean Recall@1 across six retrieval directions. I contribute to Analog Design Bench, which evaluates how agents design analog and mixed-signal circuits.

王静，南京邮电大学副教授，功率微电子实验室（PMEL）成员（负责人：郭宇锋教授）。2020 年和 2025 年分别于西安电子科技大学、复旦大学获得学士和博士学位，博士导师为曾晓洋教授。

在电源管理芯片设计方面，主要研究压电能量采集电路。我们设计的180 nm CMOS 芯片采用 37 相电压翻转整流器，实测电压翻转效率最高达 83%。

在人工智能与电子设计自动化方面，我们开发的 AnalogRetriever 支持原理图、SPICE 网表与文本之间的检索，六个检索方向的平均 Recall@1 达到 75.2%。我也参与了 Analog Design Bench，评测智能体设计模拟与混合信号电路的能力。
