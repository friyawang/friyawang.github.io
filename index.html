About Us关于我们

Our group works on analog integrated circuit design, focusing on power-management ICs, including LDOs, DC–DC converters with high conversion ratios (e.g., 12 V to 1 V), and microwatt-level low-power circuits. Our designs are taped out and measured. We also study the application of large language models to electronic design automation. Our research interests include:课题组从事模拟集成电路设计，主要研究电源管理芯片，包括低压差线性稳压器（LDO）、高降压比 DC–DC 变换器（如 12 V 至 1 V）以及微瓦级低功耗电路，设计均经过流片与测试验证；同时研究大语言模型在电子设计自动化中的应用。主要研究方向包括：

  • Power Management IC Design电源管理芯片设计
    • Low-dropout regulators (LDOs)低压差线性稳压器（LDO）
    • DC–DC converters with high conversion ratios (e.g., 12 V to 1 V)高降压比 DC–DC 变换器（如 12 V 至 1 V）
    • Microwatt-level low-power power management and energy-harvesting interfaces微瓦级低功耗电源管理与能量采集接口
  • LLM-Driven Chip Design Automation大模型驱动的芯片设计自动化
    • Cross-modal representation and retrieval of analog circuits模拟电路的跨模态表示与检索
    • AI agents for analog circuit sizing and optimization智能体驱动的模拟电路参数设计与优化
    • LLM-based RTL optimization and verification基于大模型的 RTL 优化与验证
