About Us关于我们

We work in the Power Micro-Electronics Lab (PMEL) at Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications. We design and test analog chips and study how to put that design experience to use in circuit design automation.我们在南京邮电大学功率微电子实验室（PMEL）开展研究，设计和测试模拟芯片，也探索如何将这些设计经验用于电路设计自动化。

Research interests研究方向

  1. Power management and analog IC design. We study rectifiers and switched-capacitor DC–DC converters for piezoelectric energy harvesting, with a focus on collecting energy from multiple sources and regulating the supply to multiple loads.电源管理与模拟芯片设计。研究面向压电能量采集的整流器和开关电容 DC–DC 转换器，关注如何从多路输入高效收集能量，并为不同负载提供稳定的输出。
  2. LLM-driven chip design automation. We study how models can retrieve reusable circuit designs from schematics, netlists, and text, and how simulation can guide circuit optimization and assess whether a generated design meets its specifications.大模型驱动的芯片设计自动化。研究如何从原理图、网表和文本中检索可复用的电路设计，以及如何利用仿真结果指导电路优化、评估大模型生成的电路能否达到设计指标。

Biography个人简介

I am an associate professor in the School of Integrated Circuit Science and Engineering at Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications. I received my bachelor's degree in microelectronics from Xidian University in 2020 and my Ph.D. from Fudan University in 2025, where I studied integrated circuit and system design under Prof. Xiaoyang Zeng. In July 2025, I joined the Power Micro-Electronics Lab (PMEL), led by Prof. Yufeng Guo.

My research began with circuits for piezoelectric energy harvesting. I have designed, taped out, and tested several 180 nm CMOS power-management chips. My recent work draws on this experience to study how models can retrieve analog circuits and how to verify the designs they generate. I have published as first author in JSSC, TVLSI, and TCAS-II and as a corresponding author in TPEL and at ICCAD.

王静，南京邮电大学集成电路科学与工程学院副教授。2020 年毕业于西安电子科技大学微电子科学与工程专业，2025 年获复旦大学博士学位，师从曾晓洋教授，从事集成电路与系统设计研究。同年 7 月加入郭宇锋教授负责的功率微电子实验室（PMEL）。

早期研究聚焦压电能量采集电路，完成了多款 180 nm CMOS 电源管理芯片的设计、流片与实测。近年来，结合这些设计经验，进一步研究如何利用大模型检索模拟电路、验证生成的电路设计。以第一作者在 JSSC、TVLSI、TCAS-II 发表论文，并担任 TPEL 和 ICCAD 论文的通讯作者。
